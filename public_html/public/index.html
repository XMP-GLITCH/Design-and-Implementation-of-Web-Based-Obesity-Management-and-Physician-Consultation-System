How GLP-1 Works

A simple, prescription-only process. No online checkout—just education, intake, and a physician consultation.

1

View medications & pricing

Browse available GLP-1 options and choose the medication + dosage you want to discuss with the physician.

2

Complete the intake form

Enter your contact details (name, email, phone, address) so the physician can review your request and follow up.

3

Speak with the physician

Choose to wait for a call or schedule a time. You’ll discuss goals, eligibility, and the best treatment plan.

4

Prescription decision

If medically appropriate, the physician approves a prescription. This site does not process payment or ship medication.
