What is ObesityCare Cameroon?

Medical Consultation Platform

Professional weight management platform designed to connect you with licensed Cameroonian physicians for personalized care.

Educational Resource

Educational product display—no direct medication sales. We provide information to help you make informed decisions.

Appointment-Based Care

Appointment-based physician communication (via call or WhatsApp) ensuring you receive proper medical guidance throughout your journey.

How the Process Works

  1. Browse GLP-1 options
  2. Submit consultation request
  3. Complete medical intake form
  4. Physician review
  5. Consultation call / WhatsApp connection
  6. Prescription issued if approved
  7. Clinic handles fulfillment

Fulfillment & Delivery in Cameroon

We partner with certified local pharmacies and clinic networks in major cities to ensure prescription medications are handled safely. Following your physician approval:

  • Agency Station Delivery: For patients outside Douala and Yaoundé, secure cold-chain packaging is sent via trusted regional transit services (e.g., Finexs, Touristique, or Buca Voyages) to regional centers across all 10 administrative regions.
  • Home/Office Delivery: Available via local dispatch riders within Douala and Yaoundé.
  • In-Person Pickup: Patients may pick up prescriptions directly from affiliated partner medical offices in Douala (Akwa) and Yaoundé (Bastos).
